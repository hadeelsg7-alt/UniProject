      --.--sWaiting for clicks…

      Dead click

      A click that causes no visible change on the page.

      Rage click

      Several fast clicks on the same small area. A strong frustration sign.

      Excessive scrolling

      Scrolling up and down more than expected, as if searching for something.

      Quick back

      Leaving a page and returning right away, often after a wrong link.

      JavaScript errors

      Script errors captured in the session, linked to the moment they happened.

      Error click

      A click that is followed by a JavaScript error.

      Summary
      
          
      Why Clarity matters for digital services
      
          
      Written feedback tells you what a beneficiary felt. Clarity shows the steps that led there. Used together, they show both the complaint and its cause.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      Free and quick to add
      One tag, no paid tiers, data within hours.
      
          
      Finds friction automatically
      Rage clicks, dead clicks and errors lead straight to the sessions worth watching.
      
          
      Private by design
      Masking runs in the browser, with strict, balanced and relaxed modes.
